Direct control over Bluetooth (beta)

Talk to the frame directly over Bluetooth LE — no cloud sync wait. Needs a BLE adapter and the @stoprocent/noble package installed on this PC. Fetch now tells the frame to pull its latest assigned image immediately (this is the instant "sync now" — what the phone app does when you tap the frame).

⚠ The frame must not be paired in Windows Bluetooth — this app connects to it directly. If a scan finds it but status/fetch fails as "unreachable", remove it from Windows Bluetooth settings. Scans take ~15s (frames advertise slowly).
